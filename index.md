Welcome! I am an Assistant Professor at [UC Berkeley](https://haas.berkeley.edu/) [Haas School of Business](https://haas.berkeley.edu).

I am interested in urban and spatial economics. My research studies what determines the costs of moving people and capital across space, such as commuting costs within cities and differences in borrowing costs across cities.

I am an affiliate member of the [CESifo Network](https://www.cesifo.org/) and a research affiliate at the [International Growth Centre (IGC)](https://www.theigc.org/).
